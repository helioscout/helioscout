\
In this era of AI and the hype surrounding it, in this era of capitalism and rapid, templated software development, we can choose two paths: go with the flow and follow all the "modern" technologies and trends to be attractive in the so-called labor market; or remember what the founding fathers of computer science and programming taught us, to be **craftsman** of code.\
\
Hardware is becoming ever more powerful and faster, but software continues to run slowly. What's the secret? Isn't it that a development stack is being forced upon us, and software is being churned out like matches in a factory, which is then sold for big money by our "respected" employers. Their priority is to quickly get results—profits—but what about us?\
\
